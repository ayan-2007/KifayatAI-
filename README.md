# Kifayat AI — Smart Visual Shopping & Price Intelligence — Pakistan

**Live:** [https://kifayat-ai-nine.vercel.app/](https://kifayat-ai-nine.vercel.app/)

Upload a product photo. AI identifies the item, checks prices across 34+ Pakistani e-commerce stores, and tells you if the price is a steal or a scam — in PKR.

---

## How It Works

1. **Upload a photo** of any product (or take one with your camera)
2. **Enter the asking price** you found it for
3. **Kifayat AI analyzes it** — identifies the brand, model, and category using vision AI
4. **Scans Pakistani stores** — searches Daraz, PriceOye, Shophive, Sapphire, Gul Ahmed, and 30+ others for matching prices
5. **Shows your verdict** — a Kifayat Score (0–100), whether it's a must-buy, fair value, or overpriced, and cheaper options if available

## Key Features

- **Visual AI recognition** — detects product details from photos (brand, model, features, category)
- **Live web price comparison** — searches Pakistani e-commerce stores in real time
- **Kifayat Score** — 0–100 rating based on how your price compares to the web average
- **Cheaper alternatives** — shows similar products at lower prices from verified Pakistani merchants
- **Scan history** — saved deals and past scans stored locally
- **Dark theme** — full dark mode with red-black aesthetic and animated shader background

## Tech

Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Groq (vision AI), SerpAPI (Google Lens + Shopping), Framer Motion, Zustand
